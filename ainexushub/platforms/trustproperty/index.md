## TrustProperty — questions

 

What is TrustProperty? 

TrustProperty is an AI-native property platform for India — buying, renting, PG and co-living, plots and commercial space — with verified listings, zero brokerage, and TrustLine AI answering questions on every listing 24×7.

 What is TrustLine? 

TrustLine is an AI concierge attached to every individual listing. It answers questions from that listing's own verified facts in text or real-time voice, handles interruptions mid-answer, works in English, हिंदी and Hinglish, continues the conversation on WhatsApp, and books site visits into the owner's live calendar.

 Is the legal verification done by AI? 

No, and deliberately so. Title and encumbrance verification is reviewed by a qualified lawyer, never an automated scan, and returns a risk-graded verdict in a digitally-signed report. Packages start at ₹1,499 for an encumbrance check, ₹5,999 for title verification and ₹7,999 for full due diligence.

 How is buyer and seller data protected? 

Personal information is redacted before any model call, so the models see the question rather than the person. Profiling opt-out halts the pipeline before any automated step, every AI-assisted outcome can be reviewed by a human, and each subsystem has a kill switch.

 Which cities does TrustProperty cover? 

The launch focus is eight cities: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, Delhi NCR, Kolkata and Ahmedabad.
