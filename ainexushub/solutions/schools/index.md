# schools run on Edvation

 

Students, teachers, parents, principals and administrators, on one platform with six role views.

 

Edvation is a complete school platform for Indian K-12 education — admissions, attendance, fees, timetable, assessments, report cards and parent communication — with 20 AI agents that teach from the school's own uploaded textbooks. Every AI answer carries the page number it came from, so a child, a parent or a teacher can open the printed book and check it. There is no bundled content library to outgrow: any board works, because you supply the syllabus.

## What each seat gets

 

6 roles, each with its own view of the same platform, and an audit log
 that records who saw what.

 

### Student

 

A tutor that knows the exact textbook: ask any chapter a question, revise with spaced repetition, practise without being handed answers, and prepare with board-pattern papers marked step by step.

 

In practice · Study Mentor

 

Input A student asks why the reaction is exothermic.

 

Output An answer drawn from the school's own uploaded book, citing Science · Ch 4 · p. 87.

 

Control If it is not in your book, the platform says so rather than guessing.

### Teacher

 

A five-artifact lesson kit from one sentence, board- and Bloom-aware quiz generation, examiner-style step-marking, a class misconception radar, and a stuck-flag inbox from Socratic Practice.

 

In practice · Teacher Lesson Kit

 

Input "Photosynthesis, Class 7, one period."

 

Output A lesson plan, slides, a worksheet, an exit ticket and homework — all drawn from the school's own chapter.

 

Control Chapter-grounded; never fabricated from outside the book.

### Parent

 

A weekly digest in the home language with questions to ask at dinner, study streaks, consent controls, fees and payments — designed for a parent who does not read English.

### Principal

 

Ask the school anything in plain language and get an answer from live operational data with citation chips, plus early-warning signals and compliance surfaces.

 

In practice · Ask School Copilot

 

Input "Which Class 9 sections are below 80% attendance this month?"

 

Output An answer from live attendance data, each figure carrying a chip showing the record it came from.

 

Control Answers from live school data, with the source of each number shown.

### Administrator

 

Audit log, roles and permissions, global search, and the operational estate behind admissions, attendance and fees.

### School

 

The institution-level view: admissions, attendance and timetable, fees and finance, report cards, parent communication and the 20 teaching agents.
