How the AI is governed

The same discipline runs through all four platforms. These are controls implemented in the software, not commitments in a policy document.

No hallucinated citations

Every citation in AdvoHub is verified against Indian Kanoon before it reaches the advocate. The agent declines to cite rather than guess.

Clinician sign-off required

Nothing enters the clinical record without a licensed professional's signature. The AI Scribe drafts; the doctor signs. No exceptions.

No advice, only evidence

Agents in Edvation give hints and citations, never conclusions. The Writing Coach annotates; it does not write. The Socratic agent questions; it does not answer.

Tools are signed, calls are logged

Every booking tool in MedOrbit is cryptographically signed. Every outbound call is logged. No agent can act outside its signed tool set.

Data stays in jurisdiction

Clinical data stays in AWS Mumbai. Legal files never leave the advocate's instance. School data is scoped to the school. TrustProperty data is India-only.

Scores show their reasoning

The Lead Intelligence score in TrustProperty shows the reasoning behind every score. No black-box ranking. The agent's logic is the agent's output.

Escalation, not advice

The Aftercare Voice Agent notices deterioration and escalates to nursing staff. It does not advise the patient. The clinical decision remains with the clinician.

No voice recording leaves the device

Speak Coach scores pronunciation in the browser. The child's audio never leaves the device. The score is the only thing that reaches the teacher's dashboard.
